# Environment assets

Locally bundled CC0 assets:

- Carpet001 texture maps — ambientCG: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Carpet001
- Wood Floor Worn texture maps — Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor_worn
- Plastic Monobloc Chair 01 model and textures — Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/plastic_monobloc_chair_01

Hall, track, furniture details and car geometry are generated by the application. Assets load from this server, with no third-party runtime requests.
